Create: Redstone Additions is an original mod by [Slimik42](https://modrinth.com/user/Slimik42) that introduces several redstone components designed to simplify some circuits. You can find the original project here at [Curseforge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-redstone-additions).

# Mod Content`:`

## Verdigris
![New Item!! Copper Scrap!](https://i.imgur.com/oL7thiW.png)
> Axe any oxidized copper variant to obtain 3-6 Verdigris drops.

## Diode
![Only one way in and out!](https://i.imgur.com/04KO22Q.png)
> Passes the signal only in one direction, but unlike a repeater, does not update it.

## Conjunctor
![Two ways and you're in!](https://i.imgur.com/4tjEkz7.png)

> Activated if it receives a signal from the right and left.

## Disjunctor
![Anyway in and you're there!](https://i.imgur.com/Cf0GzLB.png)

> Activated if it receives a signal from the right or left.

## Inverter
![On? No. No? Yes](https://i.imgur.com/yghEkf0.png)

> On if there is no signal and off if there is one.

## Crossroad
![Please go one and the one next to you the other way...](https://i.imgur.com/k0TABSv.png)

> Compact bridge for redstone.

## Resistors
![Resistors too have personalities you know?](https://i.imgur.com/T2VMe9v.png)

> Reduce redstone signal by 1, 2, 5 and 10 depending on the type.

## Additional Info
You can use JEI/REI/EMI to view recipes. Also includes a minor crafting recipe for [Copperative](https://modrinth.com/mod/copperative). But you don't need it to play the game. And it's only for Forge! You have alternative recipes in place.

The map in the picture is [Alpha Industries](https://www.planetminecraft.com/project/create-mod-alpha-industries-the-factory-that-makes-everything/) map by [pointnclick14](https://www.planetminecraft.com/member/pointnclick14/) on PlanetMinecraft.
